Hands-free dining at its finest! The MVP is undoubtedly the crab roe and sea urchin kama-meshi (claypot rice); every bite paired with crab vinegar is explosively fresh and sweet. The sea urchin and sweet shrimp tart delivers a double hit of umami that’s absolutely stunning. The cubed wagyu is the showstopper—grilled until the fat renders out completely, sliced to reveal a perfect blush pink centre, and elevated with wasabi and whole black peppercorns. The fat melts in your mouth without any heaviness. The whole ox tongue has a beautiful milky richness, and the unexpected pairing of chicken oil with hokki clam is surprisingly juicy and indulgent. Set in a refined Japanese garden ambiance, this place truly delivers on every level.
